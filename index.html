Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

When a customer pays via UPI, cards, or cooperative bank transfer, our smart escrow engine automatically calculates the 80% wage share, 10% welfare contribution, 5% federation oversight, and 5% tech fee. Upon job completion, the 80% direct wage is credited immediately into the worker's bank or UPI account.
Workers must upload government-issued national ID (Aadhaar or Voter ID), trade certification/experience proof from their Labour Cooperative Society, and address verification. Federation Administrators review every profile before granting the public verified badge.
The ₹100 deposit confirms the customer's booking and compensates the technician for travel dispatch. It is deducted from the final service bill. If the worker cancels or fails to arrive, the deposit is 100% refunded to the customer instantly.
Both customers and workers can file dispute tickets directly from their dashboard. Federation administrators and society representatives act as neutral mediators to resolve issues amicably, arrange rework, or issue refunds.
